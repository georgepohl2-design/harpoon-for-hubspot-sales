📺 Get it on the big screen

Broadcast the hunt to the whole crew. Put Harpoon on the office TV and everyone can see who's on what, all day.

  1. Start TV mode (button below). The board goes full screen with big text and no buttons, and refreshes itself every 10 minutes.
  2. Cast this tab to the TV. In Chrome, click the ⋮ menu (top right) → Cast, save and share → Cast…, then pick your office TV. This works with TVs that have Chromecast or Google TV built in, or a Chromecast plugged into them.
  3. Or skip casting: plug a spare laptop or a small mini PC into the TV with an HDMI cable, install Harpoon on it, and start TV mode there.
Good to know
  • Casting streams from the computer you cast from, so that computer has to stay on, awake and online, with Chrome open. For a screen that's always on, use a spare laptop or mini PC that lives by the TV.
  • Set that computer to never go to sleep (on a Mac: System Settings → Displays / Energy; on Windows: Settings → Power).
  • While TV mode is on, Harpoon asks the browser to keep the screen awake.
  • To leave TV mode, press Esc, or move the mouse and click Exit TV mode.
Tip: on a TV, the Ivory background (in Settings) reads best.

Connect HubSpot

Harpoon only ever reads from HubSpot. The access token stays in this Chrome and is only ever sent to HubSpot.

How to get an access token (about 5 minutes, needs a HubSpot Super Admin)
  1. In HubSpot, open Settings (the cog) → Integrations → Private Apps. In some newer accounts it's under Development → Legacy apps.
  2. Create a private app called Harpoon.
  3. On the Scopes tab, tick these four, all Read: crm.objects.companies.read, crm.objects.deals.read, crm.objects.owners.read and crm.objects.contacts.read (HubSpot uses that last one to unlock emails, calls and meetings).
  4. Create the app, then copy its access token and paste it below.

Connect Slack

How to get a Slack webhook link (about 3 minutes)
  1. Go to api.slack.com/apps and click Create New App → From scratch. Call it Harpoon and pick your workspace.
  2. Open Incoming Webhooks, switch it on, and click Add New Webhook to Workspace.
  3. Choose the channel (for example #target-accounts) and click Allow.
  4. Copy the webhook link (it starts with https://hooks.slack.com/) and paste it below.

Tag people in Slack

To really ping someone, Harpoon needs their Slack member ID: in Slack, click their name → ⋮ → Copy member ID. Without it, Harpoon just writes their name.
